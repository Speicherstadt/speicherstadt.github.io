2. Scope

This policy applies to the websites of the Speicherstadt projects: speicherstadt.org, haben.app, kontext.email, schreibzeug.app, belegt.store and the pages under speicherstadt.org (including Fixpunkt and Stellwerk). It also applies to booking and billing of hosted Haben (section 8). It does not apply to the software itself when you run it on your own server: there you are responsible for the data, and no data reaches us.

3. Overview

The websites are static pages. No cookies are set and no analytics or tracking services are used. Fonts, scripts and images are served by each website itself, not by third-party servers.

4. Hosting and server log files

The websites are hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When a page is requested, GitHub processes technically necessary data, in particular the IP address, date and time of the request, the address requested, the browser and the operating system. GitHub needs this data to deliver the page and secure the service; according to GitHub it keeps it in server log files for a limited time.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; the legitimate interest lies in operating the websites reliably and securely. A transfer to the USA is possible. GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. See GitHub's privacy statement.

The address speicherstadt.app redirects to speicherstadt.org. Cloudflare serves this redirect (Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you open speicherstadt.app, Cloudflare processes technically necessary data for this, in particular the IP address, date and time, the requested address and the browser. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; the legitimate interest is keeping old links reachable. A transfer to the USA is possible. See Cloudflare's privacy policy for details.

5. GitHub statistics

The projects' documentation pages show the repository with its number of stars and forks at the top right. For this your browser asks GitHub's interface (api.github.com) for these numbers directly; GitHub thereby receives your IP address and learns which page you opened. This request sets no cookies. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; the legitimate interest lies in showing current information about the project. Section 4 applies to the transfer to the USA.

6. Demos

Some projects offer a demo. It runs entirely in your browser with made-up sample data; nothing you enter there is sent to a server. Settings of the demo may be kept in your browser's storage (local storage), where you can delete them at any time.

7. Contacting us

When you contact us by email or phone, we process your details (e.g. name, email address, content of the request) to handle your request. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where a contract or its preparation is concerned, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. The data is deleted once it is no longer needed and no statutory retention periods apply.

8. Hosted Haben

If you book hosted Haben (offered in German), we process the details from your booking as controller: name, company, address, email address, VAT identification number, the name you chose for your instance and the payment data. We need them to set up the instance, send you the access details, bill you and answer questions about operation. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of the contract), and Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR for keeping the invoices.

Payment is handled by Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe, Limited, 1 Grand Canal Street Lower, Grand Canal Dock, Dublin, Ireland). You enter your details on a payment page run by Stripe; Stripe processes them for the payment, the invoice and the customer portal and may transfer them to affiliated companies in the USA. We do not see the full payment data (card number, IBAN). Details are in Stripe's privacy policy.

Emails about your booking go through our mailbox at netcup (netcup GmbH, Daimlerstraße 25, 76185 Karlsruhe, Germany). We keep the booking and billing details for as long as the statutory retention periods for business records require (Sec. 147 German Fiscal Code, Sec. 257 German Commercial Code) and delete them afterwards.

The data you store in your instance (bookings, receipts, contacts) is not processed for our own purposes but on your behalf. The data processing agreement (in German) applies; it also names the providers of server and backup. You are responsible for this data.

9. External links

The websites link to external services such as GitHub, Docker Hub or Discord. Data is transferred to the respective provider only when you click such a link; their privacy policies apply there.

10. Your rights

You have the right at any time to access your stored data (Art. 15 GDPR), to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR) and erasure (Art. 17 GDPR), to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR), to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR) and to object to processing (Art. 21 GDPR). You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information Baden-Württemberg.
