Partners

For IT service providers and hosters

The software is free and open source. At businesses, partners set it up and look after it, and the developers work with them directly.

Who it is for

IT service providers and hosters who set up and look after Kontext, Haben, Schreibzeug, Belegt, Stellwerk or Fixpunkt for their customers. For Haben, also tax advisers who look after their clients with it.

Why it pays off

No licence fees per user

What you charge for setup and support stays with you. Instead of passing on Microsoft 365 or Lexware Office subscriptions, you sell your own service.

Data in-house

Your customers get mail, office, archive and accounting on their own server or on yours, without someone else’s cloud.

One working environment

The apps work together: one account for mail and office, e-invoices from accounting into the archive. You offer a complete workplace, not single tools.

Obligations that need looking after

GoBD, ELSTER and e-invoicing are topics where businesses want someone who knows them and takes care of them.

What partners get

You may set up and run the apps without us, they are open source. As a partner, you also get:

Customer referrals

We list you among our partners and pass on enquiries from your region to you.

Direct line

We talk through problems at a customer’s directly with you, including logs and data that don’t belong in a public issue.

Haben under your name

In preparation: you offer Haben with your logo and colours. The notice “Haben by Tristan Teufel” with a link to the source code stays, as the licence requires.

Help with moving

Help moving your customers over, for example from Microsoft 365 to Kontext, from Lexware Office to Haben or from Paperless-ngx to Belegt.

Development on request

If a customer needs a feature, we build it at a partner rate, and it goes into the open code.

Hosted by us or run by you

There are two ways to work together:

Hosted by us

We run the app for your customers, with updates and backups. You get a 30% partner discount on the hosting price, set the price for your customers yourself and look after them. Hosted Haben (page in German) can be booked directly, the other apps are hosted on request.

Run by you

You run the apps for your customers yourself, on your server or on their premises, and we support you as a partner.

For Haben the way also decides how returns reach ELSTER, the German tax portal: if we host, your customers submit with our manufacturer ID. If you run Haben yourself or modify it, the tax authorities regard you as the software manufacturer, and you apply for your own manufacturer ID at the Bavarian State Tax Office. Until you have it, your customers file through Mein ELSTER; Haben prepares the data for that (Abgabe an ELSTER, in German).

Independent of us

Your customers don’t depend on us. The code is under the AGPL and stays free. From hosted Haben you can get a complete backup with the instance key on request and keep running it on your own server; to file with ELSTER you then need your own manufacturer ID (see above).

Single companies

We answer questions about using the apps in public, in the projects’ GitHub repositories, so everyone benefits. If you’d rather hand over setup or operation for your company, a partner can take it on, or use hosted operation; larger rollouts we also take on as a paid project.

Become a partner

To try things out you get a free demo instance. If you become a partner now, you pay no partner fee in your first year and have a say in where the apps are heading. In return we may name you as a reference, and you tell us what your customers are missing. From the second year you pay a fixed yearly fee. You’ll know the amount at least three months in advance and can leave at no cost. Write to info@teufel-it.de and tell us your company’s name, how many customers you look after and which apps you’re interested in.
