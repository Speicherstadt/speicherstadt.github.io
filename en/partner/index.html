Partners

For IT service providers and hosters

The software is free and open source. At businesses, partners set it up and look after it, and the developers work with them directly.

Who it is for

IT service providers and hosters who set up and look after Kontext, Haben, Schreibzeug, Belegt, Stellwerk or Fixpunkt for their customers. For Haben, also tax advisers who look after their clients with it.

What partners get

Direct line

A direct line to the developers.

Releases in advance

Notice of new versions before they go out.

Onboarding

An introduction to setting up, updating and running the apps.

Bugs first

Bugs that partners report are worked on first.

Hosted operation

On request, the apps are also available as hosted operation, without a server of your own. Partners can use it for their customers: you look after your customers, we take care of servers, updates and backups.

Single companies

We answer questions about using the apps in public, in the projects’ GitHub repositories, so everyone benefits. If you’d rather hand over setup or operation for your company, a partner can take it on, or use hosted operation; larger rollouts we also take on as a paid project.

Become a partner

The partnership is paid; the price depends on the number of your customers. Write to info@teufel-it.de and tell us your company’s name, how many customers you look after and which apps you’re interested in.
