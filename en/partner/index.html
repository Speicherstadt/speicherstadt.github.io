Partners

For IT service providers and hosters

Kontext, Haben, Schreibzeug, Belegt, Stellwerk and Soll are free and open source. Partners set them up and look after them at businesses, and for Haben tax advisers do so for their clients. We work with partners directly.

Why it pays off

No licence fees per user

What you charge for setup and support stays with you. Instead of passing on someone else’s subscriptions, you sell your own service.

Data in-house

Your customers get mail, office, archive and accounting on their own server or on yours, without someone else’s cloud.

One working environment

The apps work together: one account for mail and office, e-invoices from accounting into the archive. You offer a complete workplace, not single tools.

Obligations that need looking after

GoBD, ELSTER and e-invoicing are topics where businesses want someone who knows them and takes care of them.

Open, not a black box

For you and your customers this is the biggest difference from closed software:

Everything visible

All the code is public. You and your customers can check at any time what happens to the data, including for data protection and GoBD.

Have a say

Wishes and bugs go into the public repositories. Partners help decide where the apps are heading, and whatever gets built benefits everyone.

No lock-in

The code is under the AGPL and stays free. Nobody can raise prices, drop features or discontinue a product. From hosted Haben you can get a complete backup with the instance key on request and keep running it yourself.

What partners get

You may set up and run the apps without us, they are open source. As a partner, you also get:

Customer referrals

We list you among our partners and pass on enquiries from your region to you.

Direct line

We talk through problems at a customer’s directly with you, including logs and data that don’t belong in a public issue.

Haben under your name

In preparation: you offer Haben with your logo and colours. The notice “Haben by Tristan Teufel” with a link to the source code stays, as the licence requires.

Help with moving

We help you bring your customers’ data over from the software they use today.

Development on request

If a customer needs a feature, we build it at a partner rate, and it goes into the open code.

Hosted by us or run by you

There are two ways to work together:

Hosted by us

We run the app for your customers, with updates and backups. You get a 30% partner discount on the hosting price, set the price for your customers yourself and look after them. Hosted Haben (page in German) can be booked directly, the other apps are hosted on request.

Run by you

You run the apps for your customers yourself, on your server or on their premises, and we support you as a partner.

For Haben the way also decides how returns reach ELSTER, the German tax portal: if we host, your customers use our manufacturer ID. If you run Haben yourself, you need your own (details on filing with ELSTER, in German).

Single companies

We answer questions about using the apps in public, in the GitHub repositories. If you’d rather hand over setup or operation, a partner can take it on, or use hosted operation.

Become a partner

  • To try things out you get a free demo instance.
  • If you become a partner now, you pay no partner fee in your first year and have a say in development.
  • In return we may name you as a reference, and you tell us what your customers are missing.
  • From the second year there is a yearly fee, depending on how much support you need.

Write to info@teufel-it.de: your company’s name, how many customers you look after and which apps you’re interested in.
